---
title: Compound Learning Capture
description: >
  Self-improvement scan after Trak completion. Captures what was confusing,
  what took time, and places learnings at the source.
---

After completing a Trak, scan for anything genuinely surprising, confusing, or
non-obvious that emerged during implementation.

Ask: What would have saved me 30 minutes if I had known it upfront? What assumption
turned out to be wrong? What pattern was harder to find than it should have been?

Place learnings at the source — update CLAUDE.md files in the directories where the
confusion arose, not in a separate document. A note in the right CLAUDE.md becomes
a signal for the next agent working in that area.

Only capture what is durable and reusable. Skip: task-specific details, decisions
that are obvious from the code, things already documented.

Format learnings as concrete guidance, not observations. "The X pattern requires Y"
is useful. "I was confused about X" is not.
